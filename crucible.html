How Crucible works

Every pixel is a cell holding one material, one temperature and a velocity. Each tick, cells fall, float, swap by density, trade heat with their neighbours, and change state when they get hot or cold enough. Nothing is scripted — fire spreading through a forest is just wood crossing its ignition point, and a roof coming down is just stone that stopped being held up.

On a phone

Every shortcut below has a button. Materials are in the Materials tab, the brush and stroke tools in Brush, gravity and views in World, and saving in Scenes. ⊙ picks up whatever you tap, ↶ takes back your last stroke, and the Eraser in Tools is the touch equivalent of a right-click.
