How Crucible works

Every pixel is a cell holding one material and one temperature. Each tick, cells fall, float, swap by density, trade heat with their neighbours, and change state when they get hot or cold enough. Nothing is scripted — fire spreading through a forest is just wood crossing its ignition point.
